# Mobile: round 3 — onboarding and the whole app (the room)

September 26, 2026, overnight. Four seats reviewed renders of every screen, from the first launch to Settings:
- a first-time user walking in cold (a Claude Code developer who had never heard of Harness)
- an iOS system-app designer
- a terminal power user (vim, tmux, fzf)
- an onboarding and activation lead

Scores: iOS 7/10 overall (Find 8, Settings 5); terminal user 7/10 "feels like home"; the first-time user understood *what* it does in about 5 seconds but saw it *work* only after 5–10 minutes at a desk.

## What changed before this round
- **Welcome and sign-in:** a phone-native welcome, with email then a 4-digit code. It replaces the desktop login card.
- **Set up your computer:** shown with no computer, and behind the welcome. It watches for the computer.
- **Computers and unlock:** plain states (ready / locked / asleep) and a phone unlock page.
- **Focus hints:** one time only. The notification prompt comes after them, not at sign-in.
- **How Harness works:** in Help, in Find ("help") and on setup.
- **Look:** menus and Settings use the terminal's face.

## Not changing (every seat agreed)
- The real terminal: ANSI colours, SF Mono 13, no chat bubbles.
- Find's structure.
- The yellow answer bar.
- `[24/45]`.
- One green button per screen, cyan for what can be tapped, and the 17/13 scale.
- New's rows on top with the task and Start at the foot.
- The words "harness" and "phone password".
